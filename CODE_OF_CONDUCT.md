# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Contributors must communicate professionally, respect differing viewpoints, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption. Report concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.
